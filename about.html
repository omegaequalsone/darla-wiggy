Life as art

When life gets weird, make art. Or just make weird art.

My artistic life has been an exploration, with the goal to entertain — both myself and others. I’ve been fortunate to enjoy some incredible times making art with some of the most creative, silly, adventurous and lovely artists and musicians — both as Darla Wigginton and as my alter ego, Wiggy Darlington.

I started out as a classical singer, and got to enjoy music-making across the U.S., Italy and Japan before I hopped over to the cabaret world. I still sing with the San Francisco Symphony Chorus and the Santa Fe Symphony Chorus. I have such gratitude for the experiences I’ve had, the artists and creators I’ve known, and all who bring music and art to the world.
